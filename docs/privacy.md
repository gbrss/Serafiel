## 1. What Serafiel is
Serafiel is a chat app with a location-based social radar, used to find and talk to people nearby.

## 2. Data we collect
| Data | Purpose | When |
|---|---|---|
| Google account: name, email, photo | Sign-in and your profile | On sign-in |
| Approximate location (~250 m grid, ~500 m or ~2 km, as you choose) | Showing you on the radar to other users | While your beacon is on |
| Precise location | Stored only in a private record readable just by you and people with whom you have an accepted chat | While your beacon is on |
| Live location | Sharing in a chat or group | Only if you send it |
| Messages, photos, videos, voice notes, audio | Chat features | When you send them |
| Beacon profile: purpose, description, languages | Showing you on the radar | What you fill in |
| Online status | Showing whether you are connected (optional) | If enabled |
| Reports and blocks | Moderation and safety | When you report or block |
| Preferences (theme, language, precision) | Stored on your phone | Always; never leave the device |

We use no advertising or third-party trackers and we do not sell your data.

## 3. Storage and sharing
- Data is stored in **Google Firebase** (Authentication and Firestore), which processes it on our behalf.
- Maps use **OpenStreetMap / OpenFreeMap**; viewing a map connects your device to those services.
- Shared group music uses the embedded **YouTube** and **SoundCloud** players, which apply their own policies.
- Other users see what you choose to show: your public profile and approximate location on the radar, and what you send in chats and groups.
- We do not share your data with third parties except when legally required.

## 4. Retention
For as long as you have an account. When you delete it, your data is erased as described below.

## 5. Your rights and controls
- **Choose location precision** or **disappear from the radar** (ghost mode or pause).
- **Block and report** other users.
- **Delete your account**: Profile → "Delete my account". This erases your profile, beacon and private location, your private chats (including the other person's copy), your group messages and the groups you created. If a step fails you can retry.
- You may request access, correction or deletion of your data by writing to the contact above.

## 6. Minors
Serafiel is not intended for people under 18 and must not be used by them.

## 7. Security
We use database access rules and encrypted connections. No system is infallible: do not share sensitive information with strangers.

## 8. Changes
If we change this policy we will update the date above and notify you in the app for significant changes.
